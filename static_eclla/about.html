About eCLLA+ Project

This project equips scholars and the broader public with easily accessible and critically verified information about the worship traditions of the Catholic Church practiced until c. 1100, updating the data first published by Klaus Gamber in his Codices Liturgici Latini Antiquiores (CLLA). The current version of the system (1.0) focuses mostly on the Latin Mass books (mostly sacramentaries and missals), used by medieval celebrants to celebrate the Eucharist.


Utilizing the latest digital methods, this project provides access to thousands of liturgical books. It offers both scholarly and user-friendly tools for studying the history of liturgy in the Latin West. Through the application of artificial intelligence, fed with scholarly verified data, the intricate aspects of ecclesiastical rites become readily accessible to anyone interested in the heritage of the premodern Catholic Church.
